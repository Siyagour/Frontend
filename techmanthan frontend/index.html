About FinWise

Your trusted AI Financial Wellness Advisor for students.

Our Mission

FinWise is built to empower students with smart, AI-driven financial management tools. We believe every student deserves to understand their finances, build healthy spending habits, and start saving early — without needing a finance degree.

Features

  • Real-time expense tracking with detailed subcategories
  • AI chatbot for personalized financial advice
  • Overspending alerts and budget recommendations
  • Savings growth projections
  • Comprehensive expense breakdown with % analysis
  • Student-focused financial wellness tips

Privacy Policy

Your financial data is kept private and secure. FinWise does not share your information with any third parties. All AI analysis is performed locally within the application.
